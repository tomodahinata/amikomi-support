1. About this policy

This policy explains how the iPhone app amikomi (the "App"), provided by Hinata Tomoda (友田 陽大), an individual developer (the "Developer"), handles information. If this English version and the Japanese version differ, the Japanese version prevails.

2. Summary

  • Your photos, charts, project names and notes are handled only on your iPhone. They are never sent to the Developer or anyone else. There is no developer server.
  • There is no account or sign-in, no advertising, and no tracking.
  • Usage statistics and error reports are sent only while Statistics and Reports is on, in a form that doesn't identify you. The switch on the welcome screen starts on, but nothing is sent before you tap Get Started, and you can turn it off before that or at any time in Settings.

3. Information handled on your iPhone

The App handles the following only on your iPhone. The Developer does not receive any of it.

  • The photo you choose: the App receives only the one photo you pick in the iOS photo picker. It doesn't ask for access to your photo library. The photo is resized to at most 2048 pixels on the long edge and saved as a JPEG without camera data (EXIF), such as the date and the location (GPS), so the chart can be regenerated. The copy handed over by the picker is deleted right after resizing.
  • Projects: the chart (the color of each cell), the palette (colors, color names and symbols), gauge, name, notes, construction and chart style, the float warning length, progress (the row you are on), and when the project was created and updated.
  • Settings and markers: units, the chart style for new projects, the default gauge, your Statistics and Reports choice, markers recording that one-time events were sent, a marker used to decide when to show Pro information, and the time the app last asked the App Store to show a rating request (it never leaves your iPhone).
  • A note of the Pro check: only whether the App Store answered Pro and when. No transaction ID, price or Apple ID is kept.
  • Adding to Photos: the App asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you save an exported image to Photos. It never asks for permission to read your library.

This information is used only to make, edit, knit and export charts and to provide Pro features.

4. Where it is stored and for how long

  • Projects, settings and the photos charts were made from are saved in the App's own storage on your iPhone. So that you don't lose them when changing devices, they are included in your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup and backups to a Mac or PC).
  • Temporary files (the picker's copy of a photo, files being exported, and the copy of a file being imported) are not included in backups. They are deleted as soon as they are no longer needed, and any left over are deleted the next time you open the App.
  • The note of the Pro check is not included in backups. It is rewritten each time the App Store answers, and is deleted by Delete All Data in Settings or by deleting the App.
  • Saved data stays until you delete it (by deleting a project, by Delete All Data in Settings, or by deleting the App). Older copies inside iCloud Backup remain until the next backup replaces them.
  • Files are protected by the standard iOS data protection (they can't be read after the iPhone restarts until you first unlock it).

5. Data sent off the device (only with your consent)

Only if you leave "Send usage statistics and error reports" on and tap Get Started on the welcome screen, or turn on Statistics and Reports in Settings, does the App send the following to improve the App and fix bugs. The switch starts on in every region. Nothing is stored or sent before you tap Get Started; you can turn it off before that, or at any time in Settings.

The recipients process this information on the Developer's behalf.

App version, OS name and version, device type, and language (such as "en", without the region). A random ID the App creates on this iPhone and a random ID for each session.

  • Never sent: photos, charts (cells or color values), project names, notes, file names, camera data (EXIF), location, contacts, screenshots or screen recordings, the advertising identifier (IDFA), your Apple ID, or purchase transaction IDs.
  • Your IP address reaches the recipients as part of how the internet works, but they are configured not to store it, and it isn't used to estimate your location.
  • While it is on, each time the App starts sending, it makes one request to PostHog to read its statistics settings. The request carries only the App's PostHog project key, with no actions and no information about you (your IP address reaches PostHog, as with any request).
  • The random IDs are not combined with your name, email address, or data from other apps or websites, and are not used for advertising or tracking.
  • Both recipients are US companies. Sentry stores the information in its European Union region, and PostHog stores it in the United States. Both handle it under their data processing agreements, which include the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • When there is no connection, data waits on your iPhone and is sent later. The App never waits for it.
  • Turning it off stops sending and deletes anything not yet sent and the random IDs on this iPhone. If you turn it on again, new IDs are used, so the data is not linked to what was sent before (the error report ID changes the next time you open the App).

6. Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

The sending described in section 5 is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the UK GDPR, and the equivalent provision of the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection). The switch on the welcome screen is shown on; tapping Get Started with it on is your consent. If you turn it off before tapping Get Started, nothing is sent. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning off Statistics and Reports in Settings; this doesn't affect sending that happened before. Every feature of the App works without consent. The information in section 3 is handled only on your iPhone and the Developer does not receive it.

7. Purchases

Pro is bought and restored through Apple's App Store. Payment information never reaches the Developer. The Developer only receives the purchase events in section 5, and only if you consented.

8. Exports and sharing

  • Images (PNG), printable PDFs and project files (.amikomi.json) are created only when you choose Export, and are handed to the iOS share sheet for the destination you pick. The temporary file is deleted when the share sheet closes.
  • Images contain the chart, row and stitch numbers and color names, with no camera data, dates or other embedded metadata. PDFs also contain the project name, chart size, gauge, finished size and color names.
  • Project files contain the chart, palette, gauge, name, notes and progress, but not the photo.
  • When importing, the App reads only the file you choose and refuses files larger than 10 MB.

9. Sharing with third parties

Apart from the recipients in section 5, the Developer does not share your information with third parties, except where required by law.

10. Your rights and how to exercise them

  • You can view, correct, export (as a project file) and delete (by deleting a project or with Delete All Data in Settings) the information on your iPhone at any time within the App.
  • You can stop the sending in section 5 at any time by turning off Statistics and Reports in Settings (withdrawing consent).
  • Sent information is linked only to random IDs, which the App never displays. The Developer therefore cannot tell which records are yours and may be unable to act on individual requests for access, correction or deletion (Article 11 GDPR). Sent information is deleted when the retention period in section 5 ends.
  • For any other request or question, use the contact details in section 13. If you live in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

11. Children

The App is not directed primarily at children. There is no account, and the App doesn't collect names, contact details or other information that identifies a person. Parents can turn off Statistics and Reports in Settings.

12. Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with the effective date and the date of change. Important changes will also be mentioned in the App's update notes.
