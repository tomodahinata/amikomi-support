amikomi Support

amikomi turns a photo or a drawing into a stranded colorwork chart you can actually knit (two colors per row), then keeps your place row by row while you knit. Your charts and photos stay on your iPhone.

Contact

For bugs and questions, please send an email.

tomodahinata@gmail.com

A reply may take a few days. For a bug, it helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Settings, About). You don't need to attach photos or charts. Contact by Email in the app's Settings opens a draft that includes only the app version.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Pro?

In Settings, under Pro, tap Restore Purchase. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you bought it with. Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription or auto-renewal. It isn't shared through Family Sharing.

Can I use the app without Pro?

Yes. Without Pro you can make charts up to 120 stitches wide with up to 4 colors, knit them in the knitting mode, and export images (PNG) and project files. You can create up to 2 projects (the sample and imported files don't count). Pro adds charts up to 200 stitches, 320 rows and 8 colors, unlimited projects, printable PDFs and high-resolution images.

Projects you already have can always be opened, knitted, edited and exported, with or without Pro. The price is shown on the purchase screen.

Where is my data?

Everything stays on your iPhone. Your charts, palettes, progress (the row you are on), names and notes, and the photo each chart was made from (resized, with the date, location and other camera data removed) are saved in the app's own storage. There is no developer server and no account. This data is included in your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup and backups to a Mac or PC).

How do I back up or move to a new iPhone?

Moving to a new iPhone or restoring an iCloud Backup brings your projects along. To keep a copy of one project, tap "…" on it in the list, choose Export, then Project File (.amikomi.json), and save it, for example in the Files app.

To bring it back, choose Other Ways to Start in the list, then Import from Files, and pick the file. It is added as a new project; your existing projects don't change. The file holds the chart, palette, gauge, name, notes and progress, but not the photo (an imported project can't be regenerated from its photo).

How do I delete my data?

To delete one project, tap "…" in the list and choose Delete. To delete everything, tap Delete All Data in Settings. All charts, progress, notes and photos, and your Statistics and Reports choice, are deleted, and the app starts from the welcome screen next time. Your Pro purchase isn't deleted (use Restore Purchase).

Older copies inside iCloud Backup remain until the next backup replaces them. Files you exported and saved elsewhere must be deleted there. Deleting the app from your iPhone also deletes its data.

What is Statistics and Reports?

To improve the app, it can send usage statistics (actions such as making a chart or starting to knit) and error reports in a form that doesn't identify you. You can change this with the switch on the welcome screen and anytime under Statistics and Reports in Settings. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the switch starts off.

Photos, charts, project names, notes and file names are never sent. Turning it off deletes anything not yet sent and the random IDs this feature created on your iPhone. What is sent and to whom is described in section 5 of the Privacy Policy.

Does it work in Airplane Mode?

Yes. Making charts from photos, knitting, exporting and importing all work offline. Only buying and restoring Pro and showing its price need a connection.
